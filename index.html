Featured project

4-channel home automation controller.

A programmable relay controller with four normally open outputs, a local manual-switch input, and a UART programming interface.

View product datasheet (PDF) ↗
Relay outputs4 normally open channels: NO1–NO4
Relay contactsUp to 10 A stated per channel; confirm load and assembly suitability
VCC screw terminal12 VDC minimum; 12/24 VDC nominal; 35 VDC maximum
XT60 input12 VDC nominal; 14.2 VDC maximum
Manual inputIO27, active-low; momentary dry-contact switch to GND
ProgrammingUART; 3.3 V logic recommended pending verification
ControlsRESET and BOOT buttons
EnclosureFlanged ABS; dimensions and ingress rating not specified

Relay contact ratings are component limits and do not establish the complete assembly rating for household mains loads. Confirm load type, wiring, terminal capacity, and installation suitability. Household mains connections require a suitably qualified installer. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and smart-home integrations depend on the final hardware and firmware configuration.
